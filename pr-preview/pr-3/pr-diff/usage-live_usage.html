94> **`/device` and remote Appium hubs.** `/device` shells out to the local `adb devices`
95> and `idevice_id -l`, so it only ever sees devices plugged into the machine running
96> `optics live`. If your session drives a device through a remote Appium hub, that device
97> is attached to the hub's machine, not yours — `/device` will not list it, and reports
98> no devices found even while the session is happily driving one. That's out of scope,
99> not a fault: pick the hub device the way you already do, through the `udid` /
100> `deviceName` capabilities in your `config.yaml`.
